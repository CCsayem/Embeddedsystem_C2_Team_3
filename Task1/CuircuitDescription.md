Circuit Description

This circuit shows the car traffic light part of the project, built on a small breadboard and connected to an Arduino Uno R3 (USB cable at the top).

Components in the diagram


1 Arduino Uno R3


1 breadboard (small)


3 LEDs: red, yellow, green


3 resistors (330 kΩ), one per LED


4 jumper wires: red, yellow, green, black



Wiring

Wire	From	To
Red	Pin 13	Anode (long leg) of the red LED
Yellow	Pin 12	Anode of the yellow LED
Green	Pin 11	Anode of the green LED
Black	GND (next to pin 13)	Ground rail (-) of the breadboard
